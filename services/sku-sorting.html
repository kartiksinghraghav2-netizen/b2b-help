What Is SKU Sorting?

SKU sorting is the process of organizing products based on Stock Keeping Units (SKUs). Every product variation, size, color or model is assigned a unique SKU. Proper SKU sorting ensures that the correct products are picked, packed and dispatched for every customer order.

As ecommerce businesses grow, the number of products and order volumes increase significantly. Without an organized SKU sorting process, businesses often face operational confusion, fulfillment mistakes and delayed dispatches.

B2B Help helps ecommerce sellers establish structured SKU sorting workflows that improve operational efficiency and reduce costly mistakes.

Why SKU Sorting Is Important

Efficient SKU sorting is one of the most important parts of ecommerce fulfillment. A properly organized workflow ensures that products move quickly from storage to dispatch without confusion.

When SKU sorting is not managed correctly, businesses often experience incorrect shipments, inventory mismatches, delayed order processing and higher return rates.

A reliable SKU sorting system helps maintain operational consistency while supporting business growth.
